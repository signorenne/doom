Render with SPC m M M (whole file) or SPC m M t (current tree); org-mind-map
writes a PDF/PNG next to this file using graphviz (dot). Headings are nodes,
:PROPERTIES: OMM-COLOR and OMM-LEGEND set the colours; links between headings
become edges. The org-roam graph (SPC n r u) is the map of your notes.
